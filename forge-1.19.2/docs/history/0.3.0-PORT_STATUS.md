Electrical Age 1.19.2 — 0.3.0 preview
Development checkpoint: October 14, 2026

This is a functioning, experimental partial port. It is not a complete replacement for Electrical Age 1.24.6. Important original systems are still missing. Do not use the filename or registered item count as a feature-completeness score.

Target: Minecraft 1.19.2, exactly Forge 43.5.0, Java 17.
Recovered baseline: ElectricalAge-1.19.2-source.zip from ChatGPT Library, containing the separate forge-1.19.2 project. Its 0.2.0 source was stronger than the two recovered JARs and was reused. Previous JAR metadata did not always match their filenames. Original archives and source copies were preserved.
Authoritative parity reference: age-series/ElectricalAge tag 1.24.6, commit 17f99f7e2dd6055527bb43578af66f1d62c6842e. The recovered outer tree is a later main-tree snapshot; the exact reference is included separately as reference-1.24.6 in the source archive.

WHAT THIS BUILD ADDS TO THE RECOVERED PORT

• A loaded shaft graph with joints, hub, flywheel, clutch, electrical motor, generator, gas turbine, steam turbine and mechanical rolling machine. Kinetic energy is finite, saved per component and redistributed on graph splits; merge losses and multiple-generator budgets prevent simple duplication. The original E = 0.5 × mass × speed² × 0.05 relation is retained. Motor regulation is a native adaptation rather than the complete original motor circuit.
• Two fuel generators with filtered, saved 2,000 mB tanks, fractional fuel consumption and measured electrical output. Gasoline-equivalent fuels feed generators; gas turbines also accept gas fuels; steam turbines accept the original steam names. BCCE light/mixed-light fuel is adapted to original BuildCraft fuel heating value; gaseous fuel is accepted only by the gas turbine. Dense/heavy fuel, crude oil and water are rejected. BCCE fuel temperature/grade is not independently simulated.
• Bidirectional electrical ↔ Forge Energy conversion with selectable direction, fractional buffered energy, simulation-safe transfer and one shared per-tick transfer budget. Defaults: 1 FE/J, 95% efficiency each direction, 1,000 FE/tick. An ideal two-conversion loop retains at most 90.25% of its initial energy before other losses. Accounting tests and a real production two-converter electrical/MJ loop passed; the seeded buffer decreased through losses.
• Optional native MJ integration for the actual published BCCE 8.0.23 APIs, including BCCE's default MJ_ONLY mode. BCCE remains a separate optional mod. The bridge reads BCCE's own MJ/FE ratio rather than assuming all mods use the same units.
• Shared machine capabilities: top/side insertion checks real recipes, bottom extraction exposes only finished output, and fluid handlers reject inappropriate fluids. No mandatory BuildCraft types are loaded when BCCE is absent. Saved unregistered-fluid data is retained for later recovery if BCCE is removed; this removal/reinstallation path has not been exercised end to end.
• Original cable resistance and current ratings restored, with original loss-based heat capacity, heating cap, 150 °C failure threshold and overvoltage accumulation. The previous preview's very low resistance values were incorrect. Existing default values migrate to corrected parameters; custom saved resistances are preserved. Cable thermal coupling remains a simplified ambient model.
• Original copper, lead, tungsten and cinnabar ores with modern Forge biome modifiers, original attempt rates/heights and mean vein sizes. Natural generation of all four was verified in newly generated chunks. Copper integrates with vanilla copper; full original ore/material progression remains incomplete.
• Digital display range, independent value/dot/strobe inputs, blanking and dye color. The actual client/server menu was exercised. Shaft mesh axle pose now matches graph ports. Forty-four copied sound resource names were corrected to the eln namespace; complete sound playback is still absent.
• Forge common configuration for conversion, solver step count, synchronization interval and cable/shaft protection.
• A solver correction: PowerSource inherited an RHS callback and registered it again. After current accumulation was repaired by the earlier port, this doubled source voltage. The duplicate was removed and an analytical regression test added. Migration scripts preserve this fix.

RETAINED WORKING SUBSET

The original modified nodal analysis solver and expression/device mathematics remain in use. The recovered port includes resistors, capacitors, inductors, idealized diodes/transformers, switches, breakers, relays, seven batteries, small solar panels, 12 logic functions, nine analog functions, sensors, wireless signals, instruments/loggers, one lamp, electric furnace, macerators, compressors and electric plate machines. Variants share implementations. Working examples and unit tests do not establish exact behavior for every original component.

There are 122 placeable component variants, 23 material items, three tools and four ore block items: 152 items. This is not 122 fully restored original machines. The exact native registry is in native-content.json and assets/eln/parts.json. The feature coverage JSON accounts for 194 literal registration candidates, additional dynamic native parts, 276 original descriptor/element classes and 834 exact-reference Java/Kotlin files. The literal scanner is not an exhaustive runtime registry. Broad counterparts are conservatively marked partially functional.

IMPORTANT REMAINING LIMITATIONS

• Grid poles, suspended transmission wires, bundled/six-conductor cables, full covers/ghost blocks and original multiblock connection architecture are missing. This build cannot reproduce the original large overhead grid/substation experience.
• No complete room/air/fluid thermal solver; boilers, heat exchangers, independent fluid pipe/tank families and many thermal generators are missing.
• Mechanical clutch/slip/dual-shaft details, crank, radial engines, fixed shafts, many larger machines and injury effects are missing. Shaft mass/drag/fuel curves are only a subset of original behavior. Gas turbine starting energy must come from another shaft source.
• Wind/water generation, large solar installations, arc furnaces, miners, advanced processors/upgrades, vehicles, turrets, most armor/tools and many instruments are missing.
• Complete original recipes, resource gating and balanced survival progression are absent. Native recipe adaptations permit experiments, but a full survival progression playthrough has not been performed. No claim that every material/advanced component is obtainable through faithful progression.
• Transformer/diode stages, battery destructive failures, lamp bulb inventories, some indicators/animations, audio and many original device GUIs remain simplified or absent. The retained 48/240 V machine convention differs from the pinned original 50/200 V convention.
• IC2, ComputerCraft/OpenComputers, MQTT/Modbus and other external protocols are not restored.
• No 1.7.10 world migration. Native 0.1/0.2 IDs and saved pin convention are retained; earlier personal worlds were not tested. Capacitor/inductor state is not retained after pickup; shaft kinetic energy and tank contents are not restored by component pickup.
• No large-network performance benchmark, broad multiplayer/modpack campaign, complete dimension-transition/chunk-boundary campaign or normal interactive mouse/keyboard survival session. The client QA uses actual rendering and an integrated server, with a test-only host event-loop workaround.
